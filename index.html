About me

One person. Design and code.

I'm Robin, a designer and developer from the Philippines. I like work that looks sharp and also works properly.

Most projects go wrong in the gap between the mockup and the finished site. I design and build both, so what you approve is what you get, and you only ever talk to me.

When I'm not on a client project, I'm learning new tools, taking apart designs I admire, and building small things just to see if they work.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

A simple process so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Talk

    We start with a short chat about your goals, your audience and your deadline.

  2. 2

    Plan

    I sketch the structure and the look, and you approve it before I build anything.

  3. 3

    Build

    I design and code the site, and you see progress as it comes together.

  4. 4

    Launch

    I test it on phones and laptops, publish it, and stay around for fixes.
